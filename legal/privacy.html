Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 August 2026

MacroTrack is a daily macronutrient tracker. This policy explains what the app stores, why, who else sees it, and how to get rid of it. It covers the MacroTrack web app at {{SITE_URL}} and the MacroTrack apps for iOS, iPadOS and Android.

The short version. Your food, weight and water logs are yours. They are stored so the app can show them back to you across your devices, and for no other purpose. There is no advertising in MacroTrack, no analytics or tracking SDK, and nothing here is sold or shared with data brokers.

Who is responsible

The data controller for MacroTrack is the app's publisher, reachable at {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}. That address is also where you exercise any of the rights described below.

Health data

Weight, body measurements and food intake are health-related data, which European law treats as a special category. MacroTrack collects them only because you type them in, uses them only to produce the charts and targets the app exists to show you, and never uses them for advertising, profiling, or any automated decision that has legal effects. They are not shared with Apple Health, Google Fit, Health Connect, or any other health platform.

What is not collected

  • No advertising identifiers, and no advertising of any kind.
  • No analytics, telemetry, crash-reporting or attribution SDK.
  • No location data. No contacts. No photo library access.
  • No camera images. The barcode scanner reads frames on your device to decode the number; no frame is stored or transmitted.

Who else processes it

MacroTrack uses a small number of service providers. Each is bound by its contract to process data only on our instructions.

  • Supabase — hosts the database and handles authentication. This is where everything in the table above is stored.
  • Netlify — serves the web app. Like any web server it processes request metadata, including IP addresses, to deliver pages.
  • RevenueCat, together with the Apple App Store or Google Play — process purchases and tell us whether your subscription is active.
  • Open Food Facts and USDA FoodData Central — supply food and nutrition data. See below.

Food lookups

Searching for a food queries two kinds of source. National food-composition tables — ANSES-Ciqual (France), CoFID (United Kingdom) and CREA (Italy) — are held in our own database, so those results are found without your search term leaving our servers at all. The organisations that publish those tables receive nothing from us and are not involved in your searches.

The same search, and any barcode you scan, is also sent to Open Food Facts and USDA FoodData Central so they can answer. That request goes through our own server, which means those two databases receive the search term or barcode and not your account, your device, or your IP address. Your logs are never sent to them.

Foods you choose to share

“My Foods” has an explicit control for sharing a custom food with the community. A food you share becomes visible to other MacroTrack users — its name, serving size and nutrition values, and nothing about you. Sharing is off by default and reversible: unsharing removes it from community search.

Purchases

Paid subscriptions are billed by Apple or Google, not by us. Payment details are handled entirely by the store and never reach MacroTrack. What we receive is the outcome — which product was bought, whether it is still active, and when it renews — which is what unlocks paid features on your other devices.

How long it is kept

Your data is kept while your account exists. Deleting your account deletes it, permanently and immediately — see below. Anonymous guest accounts that are never registered are removed once they have been inactive for 12 months.

Two things deliberately survive account deletion: custom foods you explicitly shared with the community stay available to the people using them, since they carry no personal data and other users' logs reference them; and records we are required to keep, such as a store's own purchase records, remain with that store under its policy.

Your rights

Under the GDPR and equivalent laws you have the right to access, correct, export, restrict, object to, or erase your data, and to complain to your local data protection authority.

Most of these you can exercise directly in the app without asking anyone: every screen that shows your data also edits it, and Profile → Delete account erases everything. For anything else, write to {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} and we will respond within 30 days.

Where the data is

Data is stored on our providers' servers, which may be outside your country. Where data leaves the European Economic Area, it is transferred under the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or an equivalent safeguard.

Children

MacroTrack is not directed at children. Do not use it, or create an account, if you are under 13 (or the higher age of digital consent in your country, where one applies). If you believe a child has created an account, write to us and we will delete it.

Security

All traffic is encrypted in transit. Every table holding your data is protected by row-level security rules enforced by the database itself, so one account cannot read another's rows even if the app were wrong about what to ask for. Subscription entitlements are writable only by our server, never by an app on a device.

Changes
